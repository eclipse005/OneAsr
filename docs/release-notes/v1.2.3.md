# OneAsr 1.2.3 — subtitle polish

## New

- Subtitle polish is off by default, in the output section. When it is on, periods and commas in a Chinese cue become spaces, and a space is added between Chinese characters and English words or digits. Punctuation stays next to the word or number beside it. An English-only cue is left unchanged.
- Resting on a setting title shows a short explanation of that setting.
- `oneasr-cli align` accepts `--beautify` for the same polish pass.

# OneAsr 1.2.3 — 字幕美化

## 新增

- 字幕美化默认关闭，放在输出设置里。打开后，中文句子里的句号和逗号会换成空格，汉字与英文单词、数字之间也会加上空格。标点仍紧挨旁边的词或数字。纯英文的一句保持原样。
- 鼠标停在设置标题上，会看到这项设置的简短说明。
- 命令行 `oneasr-cli align` 可用 `--beautify` 做同样的处理。
